Crowd Cannon: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Crowd Cannon ("the game") is made by Tejas Telkar. This policy explains what the game does with information. The short version: the game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

Information the game keeps on your device

The game saves your progress (levels, stars, coins, items you unlocked), your settings (sound, music, vibration, easy mode) and the name you type in at the start. This is stored only in the game's own storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts or sign-up
  • No advertising and no advertising IDs
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo access
  • No chat or sharing with other players
  • No in-app purchases or real-money gambling. The daily spin only gives in-game coins.

The game asks for the "vibrate" permission only so it can make your phone buzz during play. You can turn vibration off in Settings.

Children

The game is suitable for children. Because it collects no personal information, it does not collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
